# Measured Hindi caption-fit guidance

ADVISORY, not a hard maximum or a guarantee. Repeated widest tested independent letter; reserve peak 1.1 pop scale and stroke; two lines configured. Spaces and joined/combined graphemes have different widths. Creator auto-shrink can fit longer strings.

Preset: Hormozi Style. Font: Poppins ExtraBold. This is a conservative widest-letter calibration before automatic shrink, not a codepoint/grapheme hard limit. It does not certify every font/preset or composition.

| Ratio | Test resolution | Size 100 | Size 120 | Size 140 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | 7 | 6 | 5 |
| 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 | 13 | 11 | 10 |
| 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | 14 | 11 | 10 |
| 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | 11 | 9 | 7 |

Real Hindi captions contain combining marks and different-width letters; their visible/grapheme count can differ from len(text). The renderer can shrink longer text. Final package fit is checked separately on all 65 cues and exported frames.
